## Step 5 — Safety Check & Collision Verification

Every recommended keyword (35 total) was systematically cross-checked against all 35 negative keywords across all match types.

- **Phrase Negative Isolation**: Competitor brand negatives `"any hour services"` and `"superior water and air"` were specifically designated as **Phrase Negatives**. Broad-negative match types for words like `hour`, `water`, or `air` would have created negative collisions with target keywords like `"24 hour plumber near me ogden ut"`, `[whole home water filter installation layton ut]`, and `[air conditioning replacement quote west point ut]`. Using targeted Phrase Negatives blocks competitor brand traffic cleanly while leaving valid commercial search traffic uninhibited.
- **Geographic Alignment**: Location negatives (`salt lake city`, `provo`, `st george`, `denver`) do not collide with any target keyword, as all target keywords strictly use Northern Utah locations stated on the website (`layton`, `ogden`, `clearfield`, `syracuse`, `roy`, `kaysville`, `riverdale`, `west point`, `brigham city`).

**Explicit Result**: **No collisions found.**

## Step 7 — Tempting Keywords Rejected & Rationale

1. `"plumber near me"`
   - **Why rejected**: Violates word count / match intent specificity. While 3 words long, it acts as a short head-term with high budget leakage to casual informational searchers, DIY diagnostic queries, or people looking for commercial/industrial plumbing. Replaced with highly qualified, localized commercial terms like `"licensed plumbers near me layton ut"`.
2. `"hvac repair"`
   - **Why rejected**: Violates non-negotiable Rule 2 (minimum 3 words per keyword). 2-word head terms suffer from high cost-per-click, low conversion rates, and budget waste from users searching for DIY troubleshooting or repair manuals. Replaced with `[same day hvac repair ogden ut]`.
3. `"how much does a new furnace cost in utah"`
   - **Why rejected**: Contains top-of-funnel informational research intent ("how much does"). Users typing "how much does..." are typically early-stage price browsing or doing homework, rather than actively seeking to hire a technician today. Replaced with bottom-of-funnel conversion terms like `[gas furnace replacement cost ogden utah]`, which captures searchers ready to request replacement quotes.
